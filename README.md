## 🔬 Detailed Project Breakdown

### 1. Undergraduate Thesis: Robust-Adaptive Control of an Adept 600s SCARA Industrial Robot with Flexible Joints
* **Period:** August 2026 – Present
* **Objective:** Formulate the mathematical model and design a robust-adaptive controller for an **AdeptOne-XL / Cobra 600s SCARA manipulator** accounting for elastic joint deformations.
* **Methodology:**
  * Derived coupled non-linear differential equations of motion via the **Euler-Lagrange formalism**.
  * Designed an adaptive control law to compensate for unknown payload variations and parametric uncertainties while actively damping elastic vibrations.
  * Validated closed-loop stability and trajectory tracking convergence via numerical simulations.

---

### 2. Autonomous 8-DOF Quadruped Spider Robot with AI Computer Vision
* **Period:** March 2026 – June 2026
* **Hardware Architecture:** 8 Degrees of Freedom (2 servomotors per leg), **ESP32-S3** main controller, **PCA9685** 16-channel PWM servo driver, and **HC-SR04** ultrasonic rangefinder.
* **AI & Vision Pipeline:** Trained a custom **Neural Network** deployed with **Python and OpenCV** to identify geometric figures, color profiles, and objects in real time, translating visual classifications into autonomous locomotion decisions (evade, sort, or classify).

---

### 3. Cloud-Connected Biometric Solid Dispenser (Industry 4.0 & IoT)
* **Period:** March 2026 – June 2026
* **System Architecture:** Integrated an **AS608 optical fingerprint sensor** with an **ESP32** microchip linked to a real-time **Google Firebase** database.
* **Smart Customization:** Recognized registered users to automatically execute their saved dispensing profile via 3D-printed PLA hoppers and a stepper-driven conveyor belt. Unregistered users were prompted via an interactive HMI menu to configure custom product selections, dynamically binding the motor actuation parameters to their biometric ID in the cloud.

---

### 4. Design and Fabrication of a 2-Axis CNC XY Plotter
* **Period:** January 2025 – June 2025
* **Engineering Scope:** Built a complete 2-axis drawing machine from scratch, integrating linear guide rails, bipolar stepper motors, timing belts, and a custom pen-lifter mechanism. Configured the CAD-to-G-Code pipeline for automated vector trajectory execution.
